Hi, my name is Aiden Hill, and I'm an aspiring frontend engineer! I take inspiration from Apple's spacious and minimalist designs, Brittany Chiang's simplicity, and many other people I've documented in The Source Book.


As a frontend engineer, my goal is to make beautiful, straight to the point, and interactive websites/software. I make things for the people who want something that looks stunning but gets the job done without distractions.
